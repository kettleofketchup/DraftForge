- Organizations can track attendance for their events. When an event completes, every
  expected member is recorded as attended unless staff mark them as a no-show, late
  cancel or excused. Staff edits need a reason and are written to the org log.
- Each member gets a show rate built from recent attendance, with older events counting
  less. A new Conduct tab ranks members by show rate, lists new members, and shows each
  member their own history; organizations can keep the ranking visible to staff only.
- Event staff see a turnout forecast on upcoming events, for example
  "30 signed up · expect ~24 (21–27)", and each signup shows the member's show rate or
  "New".
- Organizations can opt in to enforcement for members below a threshold: send them to
  the back of the line, require approval for their signups, or block self sign-up.
  Organizers can still add or approve them.
- Organization admins can tune scoring, thresholds and enforcement in the Conduct
  settings. The feature is locked by default and a site admin unlocks it per organization.
