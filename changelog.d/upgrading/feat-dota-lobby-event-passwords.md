Run the migrations `app.0102_tournament_dota_lobby_password`, `events.0020_dota_lobby_settings` and `lobby.0004_gamelobby_password`. They give every tournament with lobbies enabled a random lobby password, copy each event's lobby settings and password from its tournament (a series takes the settings of its upcoming events when they all agree, otherwise its lobbies stay off), store the password of every lobby that has not ended, and drop `GameLobby.password_key_id`. Run them while `DJANGO_SECRET_KEY` (and any `DJANGO_SECRET_KEY_FALLBACKS`) still hold the keys open lobbies were created under, so their stored passwords match.

Lobby passwords no longer depend on `DJANGO_SECRET_KEY`: `DJANGO_SECRET_KEY_FALLBACKS` is no longer needed for lobbies. Check series whose lobbies stayed off after the upgrade: saving a series updates its upcoming events and their tournaments. See `docs/features/planned/dota-lobbies.md`.
